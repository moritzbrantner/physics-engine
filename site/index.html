What this fixture exercises

A deliberately small but real integration surface

The world contains a grounded player body, stacked dynamic boxes, platforms and steps, plus a thin wall placed in the projectile path. Fast shots therefore exercise swept AABB CCD instead of relying on frame-end overlap. Pause and single-step make failures easier to inspect.

The current production World is still translational and AABB-only, so the player is box-shaped and the staircase is built from boxes. Capsules, slopes, friction and richer character-controller behavior belong in later engine slices rather than being faked here.
