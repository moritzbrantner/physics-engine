Plots are projections of Rust-authored observations, not reconstructed collider geometry. The browser replay shows a bounded, consistently sampled trace; the oracle's correctness checks use every observation. Differences in trajectories are expected where the scenario's invariant permits them.

What is compared?

Each named scenario is the real workload from the oracle harness, including the consumers' Rapier call pattern. The pass/fail result runs the scenario's own invariant checks, also enforced in native/WASM CI. A capability link points to a relevant workload; not every property within a capability has a separate isolated visual test. “Measure in browser” reports exploratory, same-browser WASM timings per simulation tick—not device FPS or a substitute for controlled native/CI benchmarks. Both views share a tick and display scale.
