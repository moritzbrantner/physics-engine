What this fixture exercises

A deliberately small but real integration surface

The world contains a grounded player body, stacked dynamic boxes, platforms and steps, plus a thin wall placed in the projectile path. Fast shots therefore exercise rotating continuous collision handling instead of relying on frame-end overlap. Pause and single-step make failures easier to inspect.

The graphics path prefers browser WebGPU and falls back to WebGL2 when WebGPU is unavailable. Both backends consume the same authoritative Rust/Wasm body positions and orientations; neither renderer is allowed to own or approximate physics state.

Procedural floor, wall, concrete and crate materials keep the fixture readable without an asset dependency. Dynamic crates use engine-owned contact friction and deterministic sleeping so they can settle instead of drifting forever.
